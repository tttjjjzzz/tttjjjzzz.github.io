MotorDriver V6.4 - UBC Thunderbots

Overview

On UBC Thunderbots, I led the redesign of our custom BLDC motor drivers. Between MDV6.3 and MDV6.4, there were numerous things that needed to change. These include the following: updated board outline to suit mechanical constraints, redesigned PCB layout to optimize hot loops, integrated new current sensing architecture, and integrated on-board CAN2.0B communication modules.
